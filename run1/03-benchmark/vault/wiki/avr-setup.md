# Denon AVR-X1700H setup and troubleshooting

Notes from the AVR-X1700H / AVR-X1700H DAB owner's manual (the extracted text keeps one page per form feed; page numbers below are the manual's own).

## TV and video (ARC / eARC, 8K)
- Connect a TV that is compatible with the ARC / eARC function with an HDMI cable. Set "HDMI Control" to "On" or "ARC" to "On" for ARC (p. 183). An eARC television plays audio through the receiver's speakers regardless of "HDMI Control" and "ARC" settings; when a TV supports both, eARC is prioritized. Make sure eARC is set to on in the TV if the setting exists.
- Use a "High Speed HDMI Cable with Ethernet" for ARC / eARC, and a certified "Ultra High Speed HDMI cable" for 8K or 4K 120Hz.
- 8K sources go to the HDMI 4, HDMI 5 or HDMI 6 connector; set "4K/8K Signal Format" to "8K Enhanced" (p. 187). 8K 60Hz input/output is supported and HD (1080p) / 4K can be upscaled to 8K.

## Speakers
Use speakers with an impedance of 4 – 16 Ω/ohms. The protection circuit may activate if speaker core wires touch the rear panel or the + and - sides touch each other.

## Network
The unit connects by wired LAN or wireless LAN for Internet Radio, streaming, AirPlay, network operation, the HEOS wireless multi-room sound system and Firmware Update. **Wi-Fi Setup** (p. 217): "Use iOS Device" (iOS 10.0.2 or later), **Scan Networks** (pick the network, enter the password), **WPS Router** ("Push Button", then "Connect" within 2 minutes), or Manual. **Network Control** (p. 220): "Off In Standby" (Default) suspends networking in standby; "Always On" keeps it on so a network controller can operate the unit (uses more power).

## Power and protection
- **Auto Standby** (p. 227): MAIN ZONE goes to standby when there is no audio or video input — 60 min, 30 min, 15 min (Default) or Off; ZONE2 after 8 hours, 4 hours, 2 hours or Off (Default).
- Power indicator flashes in red approx. every 2 seconds: the protection circuit tripped on a temperature rise — turn the power off, wait about an hour, and improve ventilation.
- Flashes in red approx. every 0.5 seconds: check the speaker connections (core wires touching or disconnected); otherwise the amplifier circuit has failed — contact customer service.
- "ZONE2 On" keeps the unit powered: press ZONE2 ON/OFF.

## Resets
- **Resetting factory settings** (p. 261): turn off with X, then press X while simultaneously pressing TUNER PRESET CH + and TUNER PRESET CH –; release when "Initialized" appears. Save settings first with "Save & Load"; or use "Reset" - "All Settings" in the menu.
- **Resetting network settings**: select "HEOS Music" with SOURCE SELECT, then hold TUNER PRESET CH + and ZONE2 ON/OFF; "Amp Assign", "Speaker Config." and "Video" settings are not reset.

# Denon AVR-X1700H setup and troubleshooting

From the AVR-X1700H / AVR-X1700H DAB owner's manual; page numbers are the manual's own.

## TV and video (ARC / eARC, 8K)
- Connect a TV that is compatible with the ARC / eARC function with an HDMI cable. Set "HDMI Control" to "On" or "ARC" to "On" for ARC (p. 183). With an eARC television audio plays regardless of those settings; when a TV supports both, eARC is prioritized.
- Use a "High Speed HDMI Cable with Ethernet" for ARC / eARC, and a certified "Ultra High Speed HDMI cable" for 8K or 4K 120Hz.
- 8K sources go to the HDMI 4, HDMI 5 or HDMI 6 connector; set "4K/8K Signal Format" to "8K Enhanced" (p. 187).

## Speakers
Use speakers with an impedance of 4 – 16 Ω/ohms. The protection circuit may activate if speaker core wires touch the rear panel or the + and - sides touch each other.

## Network
Wired LAN or wireless LAN, used for streaming, AirPlay, HEOS and Firmware Update. **Wi-Fi Setup** (p. 217): "Use iOS Device" (iOS 10.0.2 or later), **Scan Networks**, **WPS Router** ("Connect" within 2 minutes), or Manual. **Network Control** (p. 220): "Off In Standby" (Default) suspends networking in standby; "Always On" keeps it on so a network controller can operate the unit (uses more power).

## Power and protection
- **Auto Standby** (p. 227): MAIN ZONE goes to standby when there is no audio or video input — 60 min, 30 min, 15 min (Default) or Off; ZONE2 after 8 hours, 4 hours, 2 hours or Off (Default).
- Power indicator flashes in red approx. every 2 seconds: the protection circuit tripped on a temperature rise — turn the power off, wait about an hour, and improve ventilation.
- Flashes in red approx. every 0.5 seconds: check the speaker connections (core wires touching or disconnected); otherwise the amplifier circuit has failed — contact customer service.

## Resets
- **Resetting factory settings** (p. 261): turn off with X, then press X while simultaneously pressing TUNER PRESET CH + and TUNER PRESET CH –; release when "Initialized" appears. Menu alternative: "Reset" - "All Settings".
- **Resetting network settings**: select "HEOS Music" with SOURCE SELECT, then hold TUNER PRESET CH + and ZONE2 ON/OFF; "Amp Assign", "Speaker Config." and "Video" settings are not reset.
